The radio remains its own standalone station for phone and car use. This page also gives the companion app a doorway into the same station, so the music is available in both places without replacing or damaging the original radio.

The address is saved only on this device. Opening the station separately preserves the version that works on the phone and through the car radio. The embedded option is an additional companion-app view, not a replacement.
